  <div class="review-block-value">Mobile robot kinematics– direct and inverse kinematics, nonholonomic constraints, unicycle, differential drive, omnidirectional (~3 weeks)<br>    Embedded Technologies: Design challenges, Processors (General purpose – software and single purposehardware, application specific), Peripherals (Timer, counter, UART, PWM, ADC, real-time clocks etc.), memory and interfacing techniques, serial, parallel and wireless communication protocols. (~3 weeks)<br>    Sensors and actuators – range sensors, motors and their interfacing (~2 weeks)<br>    Control – Discrete-time model, Stability in embedded implementation, position control, nonlinear control methods (~3 weeks)<br>    Localization – Kalman filter, triangulation, trilateration, topological (~3 weeks)<br><br>Hands-on sessions will cover interfacing with embedded platforms and using Software-in-Loop Simulator like ROS.</div>
