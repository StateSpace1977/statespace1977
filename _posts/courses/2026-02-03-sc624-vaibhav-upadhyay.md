<div class="review-field-block">
  <span class="review-label">🔗 Prerequisites</span>
  <div class="review-block-value">Except for the knowledge of linear algebra there is no official prerequisite. But unofficially you would need a strong hold on multivariable calculus and real analysis in order to perform well in the course.</div>
</div>
<div class="review-field-block">
  <span class="review-label">📊 Grading Criteria</span>
  <div class="review-block-value">One presentation on the topic of your interest, which involves differential geometry (which must be approved by the course instructor).</div>
</div>
<div class="review-field-block">
  <span class="review-label">📚 Course Content</span>
  <div class="review-block-value">Review of multivariable calculus<br>Smooth manifolds<br>Vector fields, Lie bracket<br>Frobenius’s theorem, Chow-Rashevsky theorem<br>Lie groups and Lie algebras<br>Left- and right-invariant vector fields on Lie groups<br>Control theory on Lie groups, Feedback linearization<br>Optimal control on Lie groups<br>Pontryagin maximum principle on Lie groups</div>
</div>
<div class="review-field-block">
  <span class="review-label">🎙️ Feedback on Lectures</span>
  <div class="review-block-value">Lectures were great.</div>
</div>
<div class="review-field-block">
  <span class="review-label">📝 Feedback on Assignments</span>
  <div class="review-block-value">All you have to do is a project.</div>
</div>
<div class="review-field-block">
  <span class="review-label">📋 Feedback on Exams</span>
  <div class="review-block-value">The final exam was a presentation of the work you have studied and your approach to extending it (if applicable). Your evaluation (Oral Q&A) might be on both the lectures and the presentation, so be thorough with both.</div>
</div>
<div class="review-field-block">
  <span class="review-label">📖 Textbooks / References</span>
  <div class="review-block-value">Here are two nice resources for this course:<br><br>1. Lectures on Geometrical Anatomy of Theoretical Physics (YouTube lectures, Prof. Frederic P. Schuller)<br>2. MAE 276: Geometric Nonlinear Control (YouTube lectures, Prof. Haithem Ezzat)<br><br>These resources can partially cover the lectures, since none of them involve the Optimal control part; hence, it's crucial to attend the lectures. The main part of the course is your project, which is in your hands.</div>
